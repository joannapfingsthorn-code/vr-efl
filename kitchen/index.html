How it works

Learn: point at an object to see the word and its pronunciation in IPA; pick it up to hear it. Practice (once all seven have been picked up): follow spoken instructions such as “Put the apple on the plate.” Say it: an object glows – say its name; you hear yourself and then the model, and decide whether you got it. Quiz: you hear a word – find the object and pick it up. Switch modes on the board on the left wall.

Quest: point the laser and hold the trigger (or grip) to pick up; let go to drop. A / X = hear the word or instruction again.
Laptop: drag to look around · click an object to pick it up · click a surface to put it down · R = hear again.
Cardboard: look at an object (dot in the middle) and tap the screen to pick it up; look at a surface and tap to put it down.
